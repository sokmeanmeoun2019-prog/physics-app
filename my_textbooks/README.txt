My Textbook Library (Grades 9–12 Only)
======================================
Place Grade 9–12 Physics Textbook PDF files inside their matching grade folder:
  my_textbooks/grade9/
  my_textbooks/grade10/
  my_textbooks/grade11/
  my_textbooks/grade12/

Then either:
- Double-click Sync-My-Textbooks.bat (or Preview_Website_Locally.bat), OR
- Upload directly inside the "My Textbook" tab while signed in as Administrator / running locally.

This section is strictly isolated per grade (9-12 only) and never displays under Grades 7 or 8.
